# 23 November 2026 — The World in One Day

One image that sums up Monday, 23 November 2026's world news, built for the
make.fun bounty [news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123).

## What happened that day

Five stories, each verified against live reporting from 23 November 2026:

1. **Huang is back at Nvidia** — Nvidia's board reinstates Jensen Huang as
   chief executive, less than two weeks after he stepped down. Shares soar
   31.5% — the stock's best day since 2003, adding nearly $1 trillion in
   value — and lift the Nasdaq 6.5%. (Reuters, CNN, Le Monde)
2. **War at sea goes global** — President Zelensky confirms Ukrainian sea
   drones sank the Russian corvette *Merkury* off Benghazi, Libya, and hit a
   Russian cargo ship carrying weapons — a strike a thousand miles from
   Ukraine's coast. (CNN, The Times, Le Monde)
3. **U.S. cuts Iran's "last financial lifeline"** — Washington imposes
   sweeping sanctions on more than two dozen Iranian exchange houses, front
   companies and operatives accused of moving billions through a shadow
   banking network; Treasury flags $14.7bn in linked flows. (Reuters, U.S.
   State Department)
4. **Factory fire toll now 24** — Indonesia confirms 24 people dead in the
   sweets factory blaze in Banten; about 30 are still missing as rescuers
   search the burned-out building. The fire broke out on Saturday at the PT
   Fonusa Agung Mulia plant in Cikande. (Reuters, Associated Press)
5. **NHS tests AI on 700,000 women** — The world's largest AI breast-screening
   trial begins in England: the EDITH study will test whether AI can help spot
   cancers earlier, across 30 sites over three years. Recruitment starts in
   Cambridge. (NHS England, Medical Xpress)

The image is a newspaper front page: the date is the masthead, the Nvidia
comeback leads, and the other four stories fill a two-by-two grid, each with
its own picture. Every story is real and from 23 November 2026 — nothing is
invented, and nothing from an earlier day is shown as that day's news.

Deliberately left out after date-checking: the COP31 no-deal (finalised on
22 November), the BepiColombo Mercury orbit (21 November), and the ICJ
Myanmar/Rohingya ruling (19 November) — all in day roundups, none of them
23 November news.

## How it works

`entry.png` is the image (1024×1024, PNG). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard
library only) copies it to the output directory the grader provides:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

A straight copy, so two runs always write identical bytes.
